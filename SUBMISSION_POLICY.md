# Submission Policy
Before submitting your application, please read this policy. By submitting, you agree to have read this policy in full and in good faith.

## What apps are allowed / not allowed
SafeHaven accepts the majority of application categories. From browsers to notes, games and more sensitive applications such as banking.

Applications must be open client or open source to qualify.

18+ applications are explicitly prohibited, along with anything gambling or crypto related.

## Standards and Expectations
All applications are expected to serve a beneficial purpose for our users. An app that displays a clock serves no beneficial purpose as stock Android comes with a built-in clock, and will be rejected.

An application that displays a clock with the ability to create and customise widgets serves an immediate purpose and can be accepted into the store.

### Privacy and Ads
All applications are expected to follow best practices regarding user privacy. For more details, please visit:

https://mas.owasp.org/MASVS/12-MASVS-PRIVACY

#### Ads
SafeHaven explicitly prohibits ads or trackers in applications it hosts. Apps found to contain ads or trackers, whether disclosed or undisclosed, will be removed permanently.

### Application Size
Compiled releases are expected to be under 51 MB. Rare exceptions can be made for applications between 51 and 70 MB. Please open an issue for a manual review on application size.

### Architecture
SafeHaven currently supports single architecture releases. Preferably arm64, however armv7a and 'fat' builds are also supported where applicable.

Only one will be shown to a user.

### What if I have all three?
SafeHaven will automatically pick the 64-bit APK.

### Important
The first clause of Standards and Expectations will take effect from the 1st of September 2026.

## Generative AI Policy
While SafeHaven does not place an explicit ban on the use of an LLM, all applications are expected to be made without heavy reliance on an LLM.

### Things we allow:

- Debugging
- Internal boilerplate generation
- Research and translation assistance

### Things we do not allow:

- Applications generated by an LLM
- Applications mostly generated by an LLM
- Applications with heavy reliance on an LLM to write code
- Low-effort generated applications

### How to submit your app

Sign in at [ColourSwift](https://account.colourswift.com). Head over to the 'Developer' tab and submit your application details.
